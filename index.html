On-Device LLM Quantization

Edge inference on a Snapdragon NPU

Quantized Qwen2.5-0.5B across FP16, INT8, and INT4 and profiled it on a real Snapdragon 8 Gen 3 NPU via Qualcomm AI Hub. Mapped the quality-vs-memory trade-off and found Q4_K_M as the ship point — 2.5× smaller for +3.3% perplexity, against +14.9% for naive INT4.
